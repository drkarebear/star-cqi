Privacy notice

Privacy

This page explains what information this CQI support website does and does not collect.

Information this site collects

This website does not ask you to create an account or enter personal information directly into the site.

The site itself does not include its own forms for collecting student, family, or staff information.

Links to other websites and systems

Some links on this site take you to outside websites or official systems, including required forms and BTB-related resources.

Those websites may collect information according to their own privacy policies and practices. CQI at STAR does not control how outside websites collect or use information.

Basic website data

Like most websites, the service that hosts this site may automatically receive basic technical information such as browser type, device information, or an IP address. Any such information is handled by the hosting provider under its own policies.

CQI at STAR does not use this site to build personal profiles of staff or students.

Please do not enter confidential information

Do not place confidential student, family, employee, medical, or other sensitive information into any general-purpose drafting tool linked from this site unless STAR has specifically approved that use and the tool is appropriate for that information.

When using the STAR Action Plan Coach, use program-level information whenever possible and avoid including student names or other identifying details.

Official submissions

Required CQI forms and official submissions should be completed in the appropriate BTB or STAR system. This support site does not receive or store those official submissions.

This is not an official Beyond the Bell website or privacy notice. CQI at STAR is maintained by Karen Crozer in the STAR Development Office as a support resource for STAR staff. It is not maintained, reviewed, approved, or endorsed by BTB.

Updates to this notice

This privacy notice may be updated if the site adds new tools, forms, or features.
