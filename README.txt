Important notes:
- This is a STAR support resource maintained by Karen Crozer, STAR Development Office.
- It is not an official Beyond the Bell website or resource.
- Official CQI forms and required submissions must still be completed through the official BTB forms and BTB Portal.
- The detailed STAR Step-by-Step Action Plan Guide is linked from the Fall Action Plan page and is restricted to users signed into an authorized STAR Inc. Google account.
- The internal STAR and BTB training slide decks are intentionally not included in this public website package.
- The SMART Goal practice runs locally in the browser and does not save or send answers.

Privacy/security notes:
- The PlayLab coach uses a click-to-load design; visiting the coach page alone does not load the PlayLab iframe.
- External destinations are sent no referrer information by the sitewide referrer policy.
- The CAN Quality System is linked from the official California AfterSchool Network website rather than bundled as a local PDF.
- Required spring forms are hosted in external official systems; this static support site does not receive those submissions.
