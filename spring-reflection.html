Spring CQI

Complete Your Spring Forms & Reflection

Spring is when you look back at the Action Plan you made in fall. What did you try? What happened? What worked? What should change next time?

Spring reflection is more than filling out a form. Start by looking at your original Action Plan and talking with your team about what actually happened. Then use what you learned to complete the required spring forms.

Before you start your spring forms

You do not need to remember the whole year. Gather a few things first.

1

Find your original Action Plan

Look at the goal and action steps your site wrote in fall.

2

Look at what actually happened

Which action steps were completed? Which were partly completed? Did anything change along the way?

3

Gather a few examples

Collect simple evidence that shows what your team did and what changed.

4

Talk with your team

Reflection should include the people who experienced the work, not just one person completing the portal.

What counts as documentation?

Documentation is simply evidence that shows what your team did and what happened.

You do not need a huge pile of files.

A clear example for each action step is usually more useful than uploading many documents. Choose evidence that helps show the outcome of the action.

Meeting agendas or notes
Survey summaries
Student feedback
Photos or flyers
Revised activity plans
Examples of changes made
Simple rule: Ask, “Does this help show that our action step happened or that something changed?”

Questions to ask during your reflection

Use these to guide a short conversation with your team before entering anything into the portal.

  • What did we say we were going to do?
  • What did we actually do?
  • What worked well?
  • What was difficult or did not happen as planned?
  • What did students, staff, or families tell us?
  • What evidence do we have?
  • What should we keep doing?
  • What should we change next time?

Who should be part of the reflection?

Spring reflection should be a shared conversation whenever possible.

For Point-of-Service Action Plans, Site Leads guide the reflection.

Try to include input from:

  • Site staff and coaches
  • Students
  • Families, when possible
  • The principal or site administrator

Finished with your spring reflection?

Keep a copy of your final forms and documentation. What you learn this spring can help your site make a stronger Action Plan next fall.
