Interactive STAR tool

SMART Action Plan Coach

Use this interactive coach to turn your BTB feedback into a focused Action Plan draft. The coach will guide you through questions one step at a time.

What the coach can help you do

  • Choose a manageable improvement focus.
  • Draft one SMART goal.
  • Create 3–5 clear action steps.
  • Identify who is responsible and when steps will happen.
  • Prepare text you can use in your BTB Action Plan.

Using your phone?

Scan the QR code to open the coach on another device.

The coach does not submit your Action Plan. When you finish, review the draft with your team, make any changes you need, and copy and paste your final responses into the BTB Portal.

Before you load the coach

Loading the coach connects your browser to PlayLab. PlayLab may receive standard technical information such as your IP address, device/browser information, and usage data. Anything you type into the coach is processed through PlayLab and may be visible to the app creator or other authorized users with access to the STAR PlayLab space.

Use program-level information only. Do not enter student names, student IDs, grades, confidential employee information, medical information, or other sensitive personal information.

Prefer to open PlayLab separately? Open the STAR SMART Action Plan Coach directly in PlayLab.

Ready to enter your final plan?

Sign in to the BTB Portal, open the Action Plan Manager, find your site, and copy and paste your final responses into the Action Plan.

You must be signed in to the BTB Portal for the direct link to work.
