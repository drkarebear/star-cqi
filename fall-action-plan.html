Before you start

Have these things nearby. You do not need to have everything figured out yet.

Your BTB feedback or data

Look at the information BTB provided for your site, especially the Point-of-Service standards you are working on.

Your site team

Talk with staff and include student voice when you can. The plan should reflect what is actually happening at your site.

Build your Action Plan step by step

Keep your plan focused and realistic. You are choosing a manageable improvement, not trying to fix everything at once.

1

Review your feedback

Start with the BTB feedback and data for your site. Look for a pattern, need, or area where your program could grow.

Keep it simple: Ask, “What is one thing we could do better for students this year?”
2

Choose your improvement focus

Your focus should connect to the BTB Point-of-Service standards selected for the year.

For STAR’s 2026–27 Point-of-Service work, focus on Student Staff Engagement and Youth Voice & Leadership.

3

Write one SMART goal

Your site needs one clear SMART goal. It should say what you want to improve and how you will know you made progress.

A strong goal answers these questions:

  • What do we want to improve?
  • What will students or staff experience differently?
  • How often or how much?
  • By when?
  • What evidence will help us know if it happened?
4

Create 3–5 action steps

Action steps are the specific things your team will do to work toward the goal.

  • Say what will happen.
  • Say who is responsible.
  • Include a date or timeline.
  • Include any resources or support needed.
Try to avoid vague steps. “Improve student voice” is too broad. “Hold one student choice meeting each month and use the ideas to plan activities” is much easier to carry out and reflect on later.
5

Decide what evidence you will keep

You will need examples later when you reflect in spring, so think ahead about what could show that your action steps happened.

  • Meeting notes
  • Student feedback
  • Activity plans
  • Photos or flyers
  • Survey results
  • Examples of changes your team made
6

Review your plan with your team

Make sure the goal feels realistic and the people named in the action steps understand what they are responsible for.

7

Enter your final plan in the BTB portal

Once your plan is ready, copy and paste your final answers into the required BTB Action Plan fields.

Open the Official BTB Portal →

Important: STAR tools can help you draft your Action Plan, but they do not submit it for you. You are still responsible for entering the final plan in the BTB system.

Before you submit

Use this quick check before entering your plan in BTB.

  • I reviewed my site’s BTB feedback or data.
  • I chose a focused area for improvement.
  • I have one SMART goal.
  • I have 3–5 action steps.
  • Each action step says who will do it and when.
  • I know what evidence or documentation I can save.
  • I reviewed the plan with my team.
  • I am ready to copy and paste the final plan into the BTB system.

After you submit: start using the plan

CQI does not stop when the form is finished. During the year, carry out your action steps, check in with your team, and save a few examples of what happened.

Those examples will make your spring reflection much easier.
