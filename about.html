About this resource

About CQI at STAR

CQI at STAR is a plain-language support website created to help STAR Site Directors, Cluster Leads, and other STAR staff understand and complete Continuous Quality Improvement work.

Maintained by Karen Crozer in the STAR Development Office. This site is designed as a practical support resource for STAR staff.

What this site is for

CQI can involve several steps, forms, deadlines, and systems. This site brings the most useful information together in one place and explains it in simple language.

It can help STAR staff:

  • Learn what CQI means.
  • Understand what happens in fall and spring.
  • Prepare a Point-of-Service Action Plan.
  • Use the STAR Action Plan Coach to draft a goal and action steps.
  • Compare SMART goal examples, revise common mistakes, and practice recognizing strong goals and action steps.
  • Find required spring forms and deadlines.
  • Prepare for spring reflection.
  • Find additional CQI resources when more detail is needed.

Relationship to Beyond the Bell

This website helps STAR staff understand and prepare work connected to Beyond the Bell (BTB) CQI requirements.

This is not an official Beyond the Bell website or resource. It is not maintained, reviewed, approved, or endorsed by BTB. Official requirements, forms, directions, and submissions should always be completed through the official BTB forms, BTB Portal, and current BTB materials.

Using this site

This site is meant to make the process easier to understand, not replace official instructions. When an official BTB requirement or direction differs from information on this site, follow the official BTB requirement.

Because forms, deadlines, and procedures can change, STAR staff should use the links and instructions provided by their current STAR and BTB leadership when completing official work.

About the STAR Action Plan Coach

The STAR Action Plan Coach is a drafting support tool. It can help staff think through a focus, draft one SMART goal, and create clear action steps.

The coach does not submit anything to BTB. Staff must review their draft and copy and paste the final information into the BTB Portal themselves.
