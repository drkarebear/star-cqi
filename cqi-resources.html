Need help?

If you are not sure what to do next, you do not have to guess.

Start with the person who supports your site.

For help with your Action Plan, spring reflection, or finding the right requirement:

  • Talk with your Cluster Lead.
  • Use the step-by-step Fall Action Plan or Spring Reflection pages on this site.
  • Use the CQI guides on this site when you need more detail.
  • You can also email Karen C. in the Development Office for CQI support.
Important: This website is here to help you understand and prepare your CQI work. Official CQI forms and required submissions still need to be completed in the official BTB Portal.
