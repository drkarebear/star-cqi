New to CQI?

You are in the right place. This page explains CQI in plain language and shows what you will need to do during the school year.

CQI stands for Continuous Quality Improvement. It is a simple idea: look at how your program is doing, choose something to improve, try a plan, and then look back at what happened.

CQI is a cycle

You will repeat these steps over time. The goal is to keep learning and improving.

1

Prepare

Get ready and understand what you are working on.

2

Assess

Look at feedback, data, and what is happening at your site.

3

Plan

Choose one area to improve and decide what your team will do.

4

Implement

Put the plan into action during the year.

5

Reflect

Look back at what happened and decide what to keep or change.

Why do we do CQI?

CQI helps us make program improvements based on what we learn from students, staff, families, and school partners.

It is about getting better

CQI is not about having a perfect program. It is about noticing what is working, seeing where there is room to grow, and trying practical changes.

It is not meant to be done alone

Good CQI includes input from people who experience the program, including staff and students. In spring, you will use that input to help reflect on your Action Plan.

What will I do during the year?

The easiest way to remember it is: fall is for planning and doing; spring is for looking back and reflecting.

Fall

Make your Action Plan and start using it

Your Point-of-Service Action Plan is due September 30, 2026.

  • Review your BTB feedback or data.
  • Choose one area to improve.
  • Write one SMART goal.
  • Create 3–5 action steps.
  • Enter the final plan in the BTB system.
  • Start putting the plan into action.
Spring

Look back at your progress and reflect

You will return to the Action Plan you made in fall.

  • Review what you planned to do.
  • Talk with your team about what actually happened.
  • Gather a few examples that show your progress.
  • Complete the required spring forms.
  • Think about what worked and what should change next time.

Who does what?

You do not have to manage the whole CQI system by yourself.

Beyond the Bell (BTB)

  • Provides CQI training and guidance.
  • Selects the Point-of-Service standards.
  • Gathers stakeholder feedback and data.
  • Provides the Action Plan and reflection templates.

STAR

  • Reviews the feedback and data.
  • Creates and carries out the Action Plan.
  • Monitors progress during the year.
  • Reflects on results and identifies next steps.

Words you may see

You do not need to memorize these. This is just a quick reference.

CQI
Continuous Quality Improvement. The yearlong process of learning, planning, trying improvements, and reflecting.
BTB
Beyond the Bell. BTB provides the CQI requirements, training, data, and forms used by STAR sites.
Point-of-Service (PS)
CQI work completed at the individual program site level.
Action Plan
Your written plan for what you want to improve, your goal, and the steps your team will take.
SMART Goal
A clear goal that explains what you want to improve and how you will know you made progress.
Reflection
Looking back at what your team tried, what happened, what worked, and what you would change.
